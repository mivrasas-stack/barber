# **App Name**: Nelson Barber Digital

## Core Features:

- Landing Page: A modern and elegant landing page showcasing barber services, location, and testimonials.
- Service Showcase: Display the range of barber services offered, including haircuts, shaves, and beard trims with prices.
- Appointment Booking: Allow users to book appointments through a form, with date and time selection.
- Image Generation: AI powered tool that can suggest images for the website, that match user selected criteria.
- Contact and Location: Display contact information, address, and opening hours.
- Testimonials Section: Showcase client testimonials to build trust and credibility.
- Social Media Links: Link to social media profiles for increased visibility and engagement.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Mustard Yellow (#D4A900) for highlighting titles and buttons, conveying a sense of luxury and vintage charm.
- Background color: Matte Black (#121212) to establish a masculine and elegant dark theme.
- Accent color: Dark Gray (#4A4A4A) to provide contrast and depth in the design.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', sans-serif, for readability and a modern touch.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk', sans-serif, robust and uppercase, for a strong and stylish feel.
- Minimalist icons to represent services and social media links.
- Responsive design for optimal viewing on various devices, focusing on a clean and organized layout.
