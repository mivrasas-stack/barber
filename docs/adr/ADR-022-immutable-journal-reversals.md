# ADR-022: Immutable Accounting Reversals

## Status
Approved

## Context
In accounting standards (GAAP, IFRS) and forensic financial systems, posted journal entries must never be edited, deleted (`DELETE FROM`), or overwritten. Once posted, financial entries represent historical events that actually took place.

## Decision
1. **Zero Row Mutations on Posted Movements**:
   - `journal_entries` rows with status `POSTED` are immutable.
   - `journal_lines` rows are strictly append-only and never updated or deleted.
2. **Counter-Balancing Reversals**:
   - To undo or cancel a transaction (e.g. appointment refunded, expense entered in error, adjustment):
     - A new `JournalEntry` is created with `entry_type = 'REVERSAL'` and `source_type = 'REVERSAL'`.
     - Lines are generated by inverting the direction of all original lines (`DEBIT` becomes `CREDIT`, `CREDIT` becomes `DEBIT`).
     - The original entry is marked `status = 'REVERSED'` and references `reversed_entry_id = reversal.id`.
     - The reversal entry references `reversal_of_entry_id = original.id`.
3. **Double Reversal Prevention**:
   - Calling `createReversal()` on an entry that is already reversed throws `JournalEntryAlreadyReversedError`.

## Consequences
- Guaranteed audit trail preserving both the initial posting and the correction.
- Balances are naturally adjusted upon recalculation without deleting rows.
